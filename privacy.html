Being useful to the community is the prime objective of any open source project and motivates contributors to continue investing their time into the project. However, it is very difficult to assess what kind of popularity and impact a particular project has. One important source of information about the usage of the project is the information generated from accesses to its website, e.g. which pages are popular, which other sites bring visitors to the project page, or where in the world do visitors come from.

This website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, Inc. ("Google"). Google Analytics uses "cookies", which are text files placed on your computer, to help the website analyze how users use the site. The information generated by the cookie about your use of the website (including your IP address) will be transmitted to and stored by Google on servers in the United States. This website has enabled IP anonymization, so Google will truncate/anonymize the last octet of the IP address for Member States of the European Union as well as for other parties to the Agreement on the European Economic Area. On behalf of the website provider Google will use this information for the purpose of evaluating your use of the website, compiling reports on website activity for website operators and providing other services relating to website activity and internet usage to the website provider. For further information on how Google handles the transmitted data, please see this Google Analytics Help page and the Google's Privacy Policy.

You can refuse the use of Google Analytics on this website by clicking on the Disable Google Analytics button below. If you do so, an opt-out cookie will be set on the computer, which prevents the future collection of your data when visiting this website.

In any case, this site will store a cookie in your browser indicating whether you have given consent to tracking or not. This cookie is retained for a period of 12 months after which you will be asked again to provide consent.

If you provide consent or revoke consent, these action is logged along with your tracking ID. However, if you deny consent directly, without having provided consent before, this action is not logged.

Further information concerning the terms and conditions of use and data privacy can be found at http://www.google.com/analytics/terms/gb.html or at https://www.google.de/intl/en_uk/policies/. Please note that on this website, Google Analytics code is supplemented by "anonymizeIp" to ensure an anonymized collection of IP addresses (so called IP-masking).

Embedded YouTube videos

Some pages of this website offer embedded YouTube videos. These videos are not loaded automatically when you open the page. Instead, a placeholder with a play button is shown. Only when you click the play button is the video loaded from youtube-nocookie.com, a service provided by Google Ireland Ltd. (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) and Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA).

By clicking the play button, you consent to the establishment of a connection to YouTube/Google servers. As a result, the following data is transmitted to and processed by Google: your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, the URL of the page you came from (referrer), as well as date and time of the request. The youtube-nocookie.com domain is designed to set fewer cookies than the regular YouTube domain, but cookies and similar technologies may still be set once a video is loaded. Data may be transferred to and stored on servers in the United States.

For further information on Google's handling of personal data, please see the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube data protection information. You can withhold consent simply by not clicking the play button.
